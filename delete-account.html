✓

Dati eliminati

  • identità Firebase usata da MemoSpeo;
  • backup presente in Cloud Firestore;
  • metadati cloud associati e controllati da MemoSpeo.
—

Dati non eliminati automaticamente

  • dati ancora presenti sul tuo dispositivo;
  • il tuo account Google;
  • acquisti e registri conservati autonomamente da Google Play;
  • dati aggregati o resi anonimi non più collegabili all’account.

Potremmo chiederti di verificare la titolarità dell’account prima di procedere. La richiesta viene normalmente completata entro 30 giorni dalla verifica; riceverai conferma all’indirizzo usato per contattarci. Eventuale corrispondenza viene conservata solo per il tempo necessario a gestire e documentare la richiesta o adempiere a obblighi applicabili.

Importante

Per rimuovere anche i dati locali, eliminali nell’app oppure cancella i dati di MemoSpeo dalle impostazioni Android/disinstalla l’app. MemoSpeo non può cancellare da remoto ciò che resta esclusivamente sul telefono.

ENGLISH VERSION

Delete your MemoSpeo account and cloud data

You can request deletion of the Firebase identity used by MemoSpeo and its associated cloud backup. We will never ask for your Google password.

From the app

  1. Open MemoSpeo and the side menu.
  2. Select Privacy and security.
  3. Open Cloud data controls.
  4. Select Request cloud data deletion and send the prepared email.

If you no longer have the app

Email nexusrany@hotmail.com, preferably from the Google address used for Cloud Sync, and identify that address in your request. Do not send passwords, note contents or other sensitive information.

After account ownership is verified, MemoSpeo’s Firebase identity, Cloud Firestore backup and associated cloud metadata controlled by MemoSpeo will be deleted. This does not delete local device data, your Google account, records independently retained by Google Play, or aggregate/anonymized data that can no longer be linked to the account.

Requests are normally completed within 30 days after verification, and confirmation is sent to the contact address. Correspondence is kept only as long as necessary to process and document the request or meet applicable obligations.
